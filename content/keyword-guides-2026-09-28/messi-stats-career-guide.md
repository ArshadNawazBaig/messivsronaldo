# Messi Stats: Career Goals, Assists and His Club-by-Club Record

**Lionel Messi had 931 senior career goals and 424 recorded assists in 1,177 appearances in the statistical reference checked on 28 September 2026.** His record spans Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Inter Miami and Argentina. Those figures are a useful answer to a quick search for Messi stats, but the breakdown explains much more than the headline.

How much came at Barcelona? Does the Miami total mean MLS goals? Why is the famous 91-goal year different from his best club season? Here is how the pieces fit together.

The opening totals come from [Messi vs Ronaldo App's career record](https://www.messivsronaldo.app/). This article preserves the figures reviewed on the date above; subsequent matches can change them. Comparison pages elsewhere on this site display their own data cutoffs, which may be earlier.

The arithmetic is deliberately simple: 672 + 32 + 102 = 806 club goals; 806 + 125 = 931 senior career goals. Champions League goals already sit inside the club figures. Adding them again would count the same goals twice.

## Barcelona: separate the whole career from La Liga

Barcelona accounts for about 72.2% of Messi's goals in this snapshot, calculated as 672 divided by 931. That helps explain why the Barcelona years dominate discussions of his scoring record, even after his moves to France and the United States.

Within his Barcelona career, the club records **474 La Liga goals in 520 appearances**. His 672-goal total covers the wider competitive club record, including cups and European football. Both numbers are correct; they answer different questions. [FC Barcelona's historical record](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/2070529/leo-messi-fc-barcelonas-historic-record-breaker) gives the distinction.

The practical consequence is easy to miss. A graphic comparing 672 Barcelona goals with another player's league-only total has mixed its categories. Compare all competitions with all competitions, or league football with league football.

For a closer look at Messi and Ronaldo in Spain, the [La Liga guide](/insights/messi-ronaldo-la-liga-records) keeps goals, appearances and rates together.

## PSG and Inter Miami: what a change of club does to the numbers

Messi's [PSG record](https://www.messivsronaldo.app/all-time-stats/messi-psg-stats/) includes 32 goals and 34 assists across 75 competitive appearances. That is a useful example of why the goal column alone can be too narrow: his recorded final passes exceeded his goals in that spell.

You can calculate 66 combined goals and assists from those figures. But that is a count of credited involvements, not proof that he created every chance or controlled every match. To evaluate a season, you would still want minutes, competition, role and the quality of the opportunities.

The Miami row is an **all-competitions club total**. It includes competitive tournaments beyond MLS regular-season matches. Calling every Miami goal an MLS goal would be inaccurate. The [Inter Miami source page](https://www.messivsronaldo.app/all-time-stats/messi-inter-miami-stats/) provides competition filters; choose the league or tournament before quoting a narrower record.

It is also risky to compare scoring rates in different leagues as though the environments were identical. They describe what happened in each setting. They do not automatically tell us how the same player would perform against a different set of opponents.

## Argentina goals are already part of his career total

The Argentina row covers the senior national team. It includes recognized A-international friendlies as well as competitive internationals, following the [reference's international scope](https://www.messivsronaldo.app/international-stats/). Youth and Olympic records are separate from this senior total.

Another common mix-up is the meaning of “World Cup goals.” Goals at the final tournament and goals in World Cup qualifying belong to different competition records. Both can contribute to the overall senior international total, but a World Cup finals table should not silently include qualifying.

Use the [international comparison](/international) for the wider Argentina–Portugal question. If you want a tournament comparison, narrow the competition first and check the edition or period being shown.

## Why Messi's assist count needs a definition

A goals-and-assists total is convenient, but it does not capture every useful attacking action. A player can break a defensive line with a pass and receive neither a goal nor an assist. A teammate can also miss after receiving an excellent chance.

This is why a Messi stats page benefits from separating scoring, recorded final passes and broader chance creation. Where available, key passes describe passes leading to shots; expected assists can help describe the quality of the resulting opportunities. Neither should be presented as a complete career measure when the underlying coverage starts partway through a career.

[Opta's definitions](https://www.statsperform.com/opta-event-definitions/) also separate conventional assists from fantasy categories, including events such as some rebounds or penalties won. If two sites disagree, check that definition before deciding one is wrong. Our [assist guide](/insights/why-assist-totals-differ) walks through the issue.

## The 91-goal year was a calendar year

Messi scored **91 goals in 2012: 79 for Barcelona and 12 for Argentina**, according to [Barcelona's account of the record](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/1670444/-91-/featured). That covers January through December, crossing portions of two European club seasons.

His **73 Barcelona goals in 2011/12** refer to a club season instead. The club documents that record on its [historical statistics page](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/2070529/leo-messi-fc-barcelonas-historic-record-breaker).

There is no contradiction between 91 and 73. The dates and teams differ. To compare a rival's peak, choose calendar year against calendar year, or club season against club season. Comparing 91 for club and country with a club-only season quietly changes two boundaries at once.

The site's [2012 calendar-year page](/seasons/2012) is the right starting point for the January–December question.

## His Champions League record adds another perspective

[UEFA records 129 Champions League goals in 163 appearances for Messi](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a7-212c29181390-c681db1beaba-1000--lionel-messi-in-the-champions-league-records-stats-who-he-/), excluding qualifying. Dividing those figures gives approximately 0.791 goals per appearance.

That is a competition-specific rate. It should be compared with another player's Champions League rate, using the same qualifying policy. For a comparison adjusted for substitute appearances, minutes are needed as well.

UEFA separately lists [40 Champions League assists](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0297-1d3e8b524832-5bf371ab86ce-1000--most-assists-in-the-champions-league-cristiano-ronaldo-lea/). Keep that source attached: a competition figure from UEFA and a career figure from another provider may use different event classifications.

## Which Messi stats should you use?

For his accumulated scoring record, use senior career goals. For a particular club, use the club row and specify all competitions or league only. For peak scoring, choose a season or calendar year. For chance creation, start with a clearly defined assist record and add other metrics only where comparable coverage exists.

You can explore the [Messi player profile](/players/messi) for the site's dated records and follow its competition links. Keeping the question specific makes the answer easier to verify and much more useful than a long list of unlabeled numbers.

## Do penalties and shootouts count?

Penalties scored during a match, including extra time, count as goals. Successful kicks in a deciding shootout do not enter the player's match-goal total. The same distinction applies to club and international football in this guide's scope.

## Why does another Messi stats page show a different number?

First compare the review dates. Then check whether the other page includes club friendlies, youth football or a different assist definition. A newer total can reflect another match; a larger total can also reflect a broader scope. The [career-goal counting guide](/insights/what-counts-as-a-career-goal) explains what to look for before comparing the figures.
