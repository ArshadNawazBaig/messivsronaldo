**Search opportunity and constraints**

Google states that indexing is not guaranteed and there is no technique that automatically secures first place. The plan should combine useful original analysis, readable statistics, sensible page architecture, and ongoing maintenance. Deployment is the start of measurement, not proof of rankings. [Google SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

An explorer with numerous filters needs deliberate crawl controls because filter combinations can create enormous URL spaces. Keep curated search landing pages separate from arbitrary explorer state. [Google faceted-navigation guidance](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation).

Performance targets are LCP ≤ 2.5 seconds, INP ≤ 200 milliseconds, and CLS ≤ 0.1 at the 75th percentile. They are usability targets, not a ranking guarantee. [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).

Candidate keyword clusters include career goals, assists, non-penalty goals, Champions League, World Cup, trophies, age, and calendar-year comparisons. No search volumes, keyword difficulty scores, competitor traffic, or expected click-through rates were verified. Validate demand through actual search data and available keyword tools before projecting traffic.

**What $1,000 per month requires**

Google defines page RPM as earnings divided by page views, multiplied by 1,000. Therefore, gross ad revenue = page views / 1,000 × page RPM. [AdSense Page RPM](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/112030?hl=en).

| Hypothetical page RPM | Page views/month for $1,000 gross |
| --- | ---: |
| $3 | 333,334 |
| $5 | 200,000 |
| $10 | 100,000 |
| $15 | 66,667 |

These rates are scenario inputs, not observed or forecast rates for this niche. Actual earnings depend on the audience and realized monetization. Page views are not unique visitors.

For $1,000 operating profit, expenses must also be covered. An illustrative $200/month expense allowance would require $1,200 gross, or 240,000 page views at $5 page RPM with advertising alone. Actual data rights and editorial costs may exceed that allowance. No complete operating-cost quote has been obtained.

Ads, relevant affiliates, sponsorship, and optional membership are possible channels to test. Affiliate availability, commissions, sponsor demand, and subscriber conversion have not been verified. AdSense requires qualifying original content and compliance; applying does not establish acceptance. [AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en).
