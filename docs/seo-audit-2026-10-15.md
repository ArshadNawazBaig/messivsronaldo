# Messi vs Ronaldo SEO assessment and implementation drafts

Research date: **15 October 2026**, reported in Asia/Karachi. Site: [The Rivalry](https://messivsronaldo17.com/). Audience: English-speaking football fans worldwide. Work mode: audit and implementation-ready drafts. No application code, published content, advertising settings or production data was changed.

**The strongest immediate opportunity is to make existing statistics easier to reach, understand and verify.** Mobile advertising disrupts the main comparison; two high-impression English articles bury their numerical answers; and the penalty comparison places statistics with different coverage dates together. The sampled crawl infrastructure is generally sound. More generic articles or another schema layer should not precede these fixes.

This is an independent assessment based on public documentation, the supplied exports, live observations and repository inspection. It does not claim Google employment, insider knowledge or guaranteed rankings.
