## Changes implemented

1. Goals, free-kicks, La Liga and honours receive clearer headings and introductions. Descriptions and visible introductory answers use the same published data. Titles were refined in Portuguese, French, German and Dutch where the report showed relevant demand; new copy is translated into all eight supported languages.
2. Free-kick, penalty, hat-trick and assist pages show the requested metric on the player cards, instead of always displaying total career goals. Their relevant table row appears first. Unavailable breakdowns stay unavailable rather than becoming zero or falling back to goal totals.
3. Focused cards retain totals. Their options no longer offer goal-rate settings that do not describe the card statistic. Other comparisons retain the existing total/per-game/per-90 controls. Admin image exports receive the focused statistic and its correct cutoff.
4. Visible, server-rendered questions explain career appearances, club/country goals, friendly-match counting, free-kick definitions and freshness, La Liga comparison boundaries, and trophy versus individual-award counting. Links point to existing relevant comparison pages, tools and explanations.
5. Older goal-type data stays at its original cutoff after later match updates. Editing page copy does not make the football dataset newer. Current baseline: **21 September 2026**.
6. The multilingual sitemap records the actual content edit date for the changed pages. Existing canonical URLs and reciprocal language alternates remain. No duplicate keyword URLs were added.
7. Fixed 25 pre-existing omissions from the English message catalog discovered by the translation consistency check; existing translations were preserved. Refined the friendly/shootout exclusion sentence used in the new answers to avoid mistranslating shootouts as all penalty goals.

Clear, specific titles and visible relevant text follow Google's guidance on [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet). Google can rewrite both; the site cannot force the displayed search snippet. Accurate modification dates follow [Google's sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).

## Technical audit

Before edits, sampled public pages returned HTTP 200 and indexable robots metadata, with correct self-canonicals. `robots.txt` allowed public crawling and blocked private admin/API routes. The sitemap returned 672 localized URLs (84 public routes × 8 languages). Search results and the supplied impressions establish some visibility; they do not establish that every URL is indexed.

There is no evidence from this audit of a site-wide indexing block. Search Console URL Inspection and the Pages report are still needed to assess Google's selected canonical and individual indexing states. A successful HTTP response or sitemap entry alone does not prove indexing.

## Measure the outcome

- After deployment, inspect the priority URLs in Search Console and request indexing for a small selection of materially changed pages. Use the existing sitemap: https://messivsronaldo17.com/sitemap.xml. It does not need a new URL for this change.
- In Performance, filter each exact query, open **Pages**, and confirm the intended language/URL appears. Check country and device before assuming the same average position applies everywhere.
- The supplied baseline covers **7 days**, not 28. Compare subsequent complete seven-day periods using the same filters; once available, use a 28-day view to assess the broader trend. Do not compare this seven-day export directly with a 28-day total. Track impressions, clicks, CTR and average position separately for these groups. The current sample is too small to attribute zero clicks reliably to snippets, position or lack of demand.
- Check indexing after about one week; evaluate performance after about four weeks. Extend the observation period if the sample remains small. Google recrawling and ranking changes are outside the application’s control.
- Keep publishing verified match updates. Never refresh displayed cutoff dates merely to look current. Keep source definitions visible when totals differ.
- Add another focused article only when query demand and a useful unanswered question justify it. Existing 2012/2017 pages, tools and explanations should be assessed before making overlapping URLs.

There is no guaranteed ranking, traffic increase, AdSense approval or income from these changes. Google explains that SEO results vary and changes may take time in its [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).
