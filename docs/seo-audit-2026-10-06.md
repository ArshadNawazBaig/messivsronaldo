# The Rivalry SEO GEO and AEO audit

Audit date: 6 October 2026. Website: [messivsronaldo17.com](https://messivsronaldo17.com). Objective: grow relevant organic visits and useful exploration of the football comparisons.

The strongest opportunities are clearer page intent, reliable statistical coverage, better connections between analysis and records, and a more stable mobile advertising experience. The owner’s new Search Console exports add a baseline of **177 clicks, 12,940 impressions and 1.37% CTR** for the supplied 20 September–4 October dates. They prioritize the Dutch award-date guide, the peak-year comparison and La Liga, while confirming that French/Spanish award articles already attract clicks. The site already has substantial technical SEO infrastructure. Rebuilding its URL structure or producing more near-identical comparison pages would be a poor first investment.

The code changes described below are implemented in the workspace. They have **not been deployed**. Production observations and local validation are distinguished throughout. Scores are editorial assessments, not Google scores or predictions of rankings.

Follow-up: the [7 October implementation report](seo-improvements-2026-10-07.md) records additional translation-payload, font-loading, priority-copy and reproducible-research improvements, with the remaining production and account checks. The scores below retain their original production scope.

## Answer clarity and generative search

Existing visible answers, tables, definitions and provenance already provide a sound base. The changes put article summaries before images and connect explanations to the relevant current record. Continue using a short answer followed by scope, cutoff, calculation and source. For example, a free-kick answer should identify direct free kicks and excluded indirect routines; a trophy answer should state which team honours are counted.

The publisher entity is The Rivalry, a worldwide independent football comparison project. Player identities remain separate Person entities. The live About page contains the configured editor profile; keep the supplied biography and responsibility statement, without upgrading it into unverified credentials. Existing methodology, correction form, update log, social profiles and image credits support trust. Source transparency does not establish an independently audited match ledger.

Build citation-worthy assets around inspectable calculations and corrections. Cite primary football sources for factual announcements and retain provider-specific definitions for derived statistics. Mark unavailable classifications rather than filling gaps. Retain article publication dates when changing layout or navigation; sitemap content revisions are tracked separately.

Google says established SEO practices apply to its AI features, and no special AI file or schema guarantees inclusion. Maintain the existing `llms.txt` only as a convenience, not a ranking intervention. [Google AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [2026 optimization resource announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/05/a-new-resource-for-optimizing).

Visible question-and-answer sections remain useful. Do not budget for FAQ rich-result markup as a Google appearance strategy: Google’s current changelog says that feature stopped appearing on 7 May 2026. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

## Schema and internal linking implementation

Keep WebSite and the actual publisher Organization, contextual WebPage/CollectionPage, visible BreadcrumbList, Article with its real publisher/editor/dates, Person for the players, and glossary definitions. Dataset markup accurately describes the comparison data, its values, sources and reuse terms; it is relevant to Dataset Search rather than a promise of ordinary Google rich results. [Dataset documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/dataset).

Do not add Product, LocalBusiness, Service, Review, AggregateRating, Event, HowTo or VideoObject without matching content and eligibility. There are no products, reviews, public business premises or first-party videos in scope that justify these additions. Existing Article markup should be checked against the live visible content and Google’s [Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article).

The contextual map now connects free-kick/penalty/hat-trick articles to their record pages, individual guides to player profiles, year analyses to their exact archive, and retirement analysis to the applicable player/international/club records. Reciprocal links are prioritized within the four displayed recommendations; unpublished language variants are filtered out. Native anchor links retain their localized destinations. [Google link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable) supports descriptive, crawlable navigation.

Preserve the homepage as the broad entry point. Links from explainers should name the intended comparison, not repeat “click here.” Footer/site-directory reachability is a useful floor; relevant links within the reading journey are the engagement opportunity.

## Ethical authority building

An observed editorial precedent is [TIME’s Messi–Ronaldo comparison](https://time.com/6240741/messi-ronaldo-greatest-of-all-time-stats-comparison/), which attributes a statistical selection to Messi vs Ronaldo App. This establishes an example of a publication citing a specialist source, not a claim about the competitor’s entire backlink profile. [The Week’s comparison](https://theweek.com/sport/football/955312/lionel-messi-vs-cristiano-ronaldo-rivalry-all-time-goals-career-stats) is another relevant research prospect.

Prepare a short public methodology pack: the exact question, dated inputs, formula, downloadable original chart, reuse terms and the comparison URL. Target a small set of football writers, statistics educators and relevant fan publications whose existing work fits that specific asset. Offer a useful correction or calculation without demanding a link. This is P2 with potential referral traffic and independent corroboration; acceptance is uncertain.

For partnerships, offer a traceable worked example to a sports-data course or analyst newsletter. Consider a maintained football-data resource listing only if submissions are editorially reviewed and appropriate. Check brand mentions for omitted attribution only after identifying real mentions. Competitor backlink-gap analysis requires an actual link export; no referring domains or unlinked mentions are fabricated. No outreach messages were sent. Do not buy ranking links, automate comments or submit to bulk directories.

Local SEO is **not applicable** to the worldwide online publication. Do not create a Google Business Profile, invented address, NAP footprint or city doorway pages to satisfy a checklist.
