# Messi vs Ronaldo search visibility audit and implementation

Audit date: 26 October 2026. Website: [messivsronaldo17.com](https://messivsronaldo17.com/). Audience: football fans worldwide, including the site's nine language editions. Search Console observation period: **27 September–24 October 2026**, Web (text-based). Application changes are prepared locally; this audit does not represent a production deployment.

## 1. Executive summary

**The evidence does not support “only one or two pages rank.” The immediate problem is uneven visibility and weak conversion of impressions on specific intents, alongside a reproducible mobile layout problem.**

1. **[CONFIRMED by data] Many pages appear, but broad comparison searches remain weak.** There are **908 URL rows with impressions and 228 with clicks**. The top two contribute only **18.75% of page-table clicks**. However, the exact query `messi vs ronaldo` has only 60 impressions, one click and average position 21.30; `messi vs ronaldo stats` has six impressions and position 65.50. These small query samples do not establish stable rankings, and the site's approximate average position 7.53 does not describe these competitive terms.
2. **[CONFIRMED by data] Exposure is growing over the observed window, with a recent daily pullback.** The property has **731 clicks, 68,884 impressions and 1.06% CTR**. Second-half impressions are 100.4% above the first half; clicks are 65.8% higher. Daily impressions peak at 5,334 on October 18 and end at 2,397 on October 24. **[HYPOTHESIS to test]** Demand, query mix, event timing and rankings can each contribute; aggregate data cannot isolate them.
3. **[CONFIRMED by data] Several visible intents have very low click-through.** `/nl/insights/ballon-dor-2026-date-voting-rules` has 3,837 impressions, five clicks, 0.13% CTR and position 8.99. `/international` has 1,498 impressions, seven clicks and 0.47% CTR. There are 123 page rows with at least 100 impressions and CTR below 1%. **[LIKELY]** Clearer answers, scope and search snippets are more useful immediate work than adding another generic SEO layer. These thresholds are triage rules, not proof that a title causes low CTR.
4. **[CONFIRMED by data] The mobile layout shifts substantially when advertising requests are allowed.** Two throttled runs each recorded CLS 0.338 on `/` and 0.209 on `/international`; equivalent ad-blocked runs recorded zero. Mobile supplies 86.18% of clicks. **[LIKELY]** Ad insertion is degrading access to the primary comparison. Its contribution to rankings is unproved.
5. **[LIKELY] Distinctiveness is a larger constraint than basic crawlability.** The sampled site already provides server-rendered statistics, canonicals, sources, a named editor and contextual links. The main competitor offers deeper year/match breakdowns and is also a declared baseline statistical source. Reformatting shared totals alone offers limited differentiation. **[HYPOTHESIS to test]** Independent links, reputation and Google-selected canonicals may also matter; none can be diagnosed from these exports.
